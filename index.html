New Hire Opportunities Initiative

Supporting early-career professionals through non-repayable funding for firm-wide strategic participation initiatives across eligible Ontario Investment Banking Union member firms.

Overview

As part of Ontario’s ongoing support for workforce development and strategic participation, the recently announced New Hire Opportunities Initiative, under the Growing Ontario Fund, provides non-repayable funding of up to $15,000 for contracted employees of participating Ontario Investment Banking Union firms who have been with an organization for less than three years.

Maximum funding
$15,000
Eligible employees
Junior professionals
Tenure requirement
< 3 years

Program intent

The initiative is designed to provide newer employees with direct exposure to, and participation in, firm-wide opportunities that require capital contributions. This approach is intended to support meaningful integration into strategic initiatives during the early years of employment.

Who should consider applying

  • Contracted employees of eligible OIBU firms
  • Junior professionals within their first three years of service
  • Team members participating in approved strategic initiatives
  • Individuals contributing to capital-supported firm-wide participation opportunities
Applicant note: Employees beyond the 3-year threshold may no longer qualify under current program criteria.

Application checklist

  1. Confirm that your tenure is below 3 years
  2. Identify the relevant capital-supported initiative
  3. Prepare a concise justification memorandum
  4. Obtain internal organizational approval
  5. Complete the required supporting documentation
